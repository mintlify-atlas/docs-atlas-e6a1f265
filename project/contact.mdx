GastoClaro is an open, academic project and feedback from real users is genuinely valuable. Whether you have found a bug, have a question about how a feature works, or want to suggest an improvement, there are two straightforward ways to reach the team: the contact form built into the app itself, and the public GitHub repository.

## What the contact form is for

The contact form on GastoClaro's `contacto.html` page is the right place for:

- **General feedback** — sharing what you like, what feels unclear, or what you wish the app did differently.
- **Questions** — asking about how a specific feature works or getting clarification on something in the documentation.
- **Non-technical issues** — reporting problems that do not fit neatly into a bug report, such as unclear labels, confusing flows, or accessibility concerns.

The form is intentionally simple: you provide your name, email address, and a message, then submit. The team reviews every submission.

## Contact form fields

When you open the contact page in the GastoClaro app, you will find three fields:

- **Name** — your first name or the name you want the team to use when responding.
- **Email** — your email address, so the team can follow up with a reply.
- **Message** — a description of your feedback, question, or issue. Be as specific as you can: the more context you provide, the more useful the response will be.

<Info>
  The contact form is part of the GastoClaro web app. To access it, open the app in your browser and navigate to the `contacto.html` page, or click the contact link in the app's navigation.
</Info>

## Reporting bugs and contributing

For technical issues — unexpected behavior, broken features, or errors in the browser console — the best place to report is the GitHub repository. Opening an issue there creates a public, trackable record that the team can triage, reproduce, and fix.

The repository is also where you can contribute code, suggest changes to existing features, or propose entirely new ones. Contributions of all sizes are welcome.

**GitHub repository:** [github.com/sgGuerra/GastoClaro-Proyecto-Desarrollo-Web](https://github.com/sgGuerra/GastoClaro-Proyecto-Desarrollo-Web)

<Tip>
  When filing a bug report on GitHub, include the browser you are using, the steps to reproduce the problem, and what you expected to happen versus what actually happened. This helps the team reproduce and fix the issue faster.
</Tip>
