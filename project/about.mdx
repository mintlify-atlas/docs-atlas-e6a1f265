GastoClaro is a browser-based expense tracker built on the belief that managing your money should not require a finance degree, a paid subscription, or a complicated setup. It was created to give students, young workers, and anyone curious about their spending a simple, private, and honest tool for understanding where their money goes every day.

## Mission

GastoClaro's mission is to help you understand and control your daily expenses in a simple, visual, and stress-free way — while encouraging better financial habits over time. The app strips away everything that makes traditional finance tools intimidating: no dashboards full of jargon, no mandatory account creation, no overwhelming configuration. You open GastoClaro, add an expense in under ten seconds, and immediately see the result.

## Vision

GastoClaro aims to become the go-to tool for students and young workers who want to make conscious financial decisions without needing advanced financial knowledge. Financial literacy should not be a privilege reserved for people with economics backgrounds or expensive advisors. By making expense tracking frictionless and visually clear, GastoClaro helps you build the habit of awareness — knowing what you spend, where you spend it, and how that changes month to month.

## Objectives

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fast expense registration" icon="bolt">
    GastoClaro lets you record any expense — description, amount, category, and date — in a single short form. The goal is to remove every possible obstacle between a purchase and the moment you log it, so the habit of recording sticks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Visual spending breakdowns" icon="chart-pie">
    Numbers alone rarely change behavior. GastoClaro presents your spending as a category chart and monthly total so you can immediately see patterns: how much went to food this month, how transport costs compare to leisure, and where the biggest opportunities to adjust are.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Daily habit building" icon="calendar-check">
    The app is deliberately fast and low-friction to support the habit of daily recording. Logging an expense should feel as natural as checking your messages — quick, easy, and worth the effort because the insight is immediate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clearer financial decision-making" icon="lightbulb">
    Seeing your spending summarized clearly — by category, by month, over time — gives you the information you need to make better choices. GastoClaro does not tell you what to do with your money; it gives you the data to decide for yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Value proposition

GastoClaro is different from traditional budgeting apps because it prioritizes simplicity and accessibility above all else.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Extreme simplicity" icon="minimize">
    GastoClaro includes only the features you actually need to track daily expenses. There are no budgets to configure, no recurring transactions to set up, and no integrations to manage. Less complexity means fewer reasons to give up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No expertise required" icon="user">
    The app is designed for people without any financial background. Every label, form field, and chart is written in plain language so you can start using it the moment you open it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clear, friendly visualization" icon="chart-bar">
    Your spending is displayed as a category chart and monthly total that update in real time as you add or remove expenses. You always have a clear picture of your finances without digging through tables or reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works directly in your browser" icon="globe">
    GastoClaro is a plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript application. There is nothing to install, no server to run, and no account to create. Open `index.html` and you're ready to track expenses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Focused on habits, not just numbers" icon="repeat">
    Tracking expenses is most valuable when it becomes a daily habit. GastoClaro is fast enough — and simple enough — that logging a gasto takes less than ten seconds, making it easy to stay consistent.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
