GastoClaro is a browser-based personal expense tracker designed to help you understand and control your daily spending — no account, no installation, and no financial expertise required. You open it, add an expense in seconds, and immediately see where your money is going. All your data stays in your browser, so your finances remain completely private.

## Who is GastoClaro for?

GastoClaro is built for anyone who wants a straightforward way to stay on top of their spending without dealing with complex financial tools.

**Students** often have limited and variable income. GastoClaro makes it easy to track everyday costs — meals, transport, subscriptions — and see exactly how much you're spending each month.

**Young workers** entering the workforce for the first time benefit from building the habit of recording expenses early. GastoClaro keeps the process fast and friction-free so you'll actually stick with it.

**Anyone without financial expertise** can use GastoClaro right away. There are no budgets to configure, no accounts to link, and no terminology to learn. If you can fill out a short form, you can track your expenses.

## How it works

GastoClaro runs entirely in your browser. You open `index.html`, fill out the expense form, and click **Add** — that's it. Your expense appears in the list, your monthly total updates, and the category chart refreshes to reflect the new entry.

<Note>
  No account or registration is required. GastoClaro never sends your data to a server. Everything is stored locally in your browser using localStorage, which means your expenses are private and available every time you return to the app on the same device.
</Note>

Whenever you want to review your spending, the monthly summary and chart are right on the main page. You can delete any expense at any time, and the totals update instantly.
