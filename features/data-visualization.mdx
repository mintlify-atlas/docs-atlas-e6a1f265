GastoClaro turns your expense data into two at-a-glance visual elements: a monthly total that shows your overall spending, and a category breakdown chart that shows where that money went. Both update automatically as you add or delete expenses, so you always have a current picture without needing to do any manual calculation or refreshing.

## Monthly total

The monthly total appears prominently on the main page. It adds up every expense you have recorded for the current calendar month and displays the result as a single number.

<Info>
  The total reflects your current month's expenses. GastoClaro's free plan focuses on the current month's view — multi-month history is a planned feature for the Plus plan.
</Info>

### When it updates

The monthly total recalculates every time you:

- Add a new expense
- Delete an existing expense

There is no Save button and no need to refresh the page. The update happens in the browser the moment you confirm the action.

## Category breakdown chart

Below the monthly total, GastoClaro displays a chart that breaks your spending down by category. Each segment or bar represents one of the available categories — food, transport, and leisure — and its size corresponds to the proportion of your total spending that category accounts for.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What it shows">
    The chart gives you a proportional view of your spending distribution for the current month. For example, if you spent €300 on food, €100 on transport, and €100 on leisure, the chart reflects those relative weights visually so you can see at a glance that food represents half your spending this month.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="When it updates">
    Like the monthly total, the chart updates in real time. Adding an expense increases the weight of that expense's category. Deleting an expense reduces it. The chart always reflects the current state of your expense list without any manual action on your part.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Designed for clarity

GastoClaro's chart is intentionally simple. You do not need to understand data analysis or financial software to read it. The goal is to give anyone — regardless of technical background — a clear, immediate sense of their spending patterns with no learning curve.

<Note>
  The chart currently covers the three MVP categories: food, transport, and leisure. If an expense falls into a category with no other entries that month, it will appear as its own segment, giving you a complete picture of all recorded spending.
</Note>

## Why visualization builds better financial habits

Seeing numbers in a list is useful. Seeing them as a visual proportion is more powerful. Research on financial behavior consistently shows that people who have a visual representation of their spending are more likely to notice outliers, stick to budgets, and adjust habits over time.

GastoClaro is designed around this principle: the chart is not an optional extra — it is one of the core reasons to use the app every day. When you record expenses consistently and glance at the chart regularly, you develop an intuitive sense of your spending rhythm that is hard to build from raw numbers alone.

<Tip>
  Try checking the chart at the end of each week, not just at the end of the month. Catching a category that is growing too fast midway through the month gives you time to adjust before you hit your limit.
</Tip>
