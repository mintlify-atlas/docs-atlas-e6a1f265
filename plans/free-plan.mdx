The Free plan is the current GastoClaro release — fully implemented and available right now. It gives you everything you need to record daily expenses, organize them by category, and review your monthly spending. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no payment required.

<Note>
  All data is stored in localStorage — no account or payment required. Your expenses persist between sessions on the same device automatically.
</Note>

## What is included

**Manual expense registration**
You can add an expense at any time using the registration form. Each entry records a description, amount, category, and date. Fields are validated before saving so incomplete entries are never stored.

**Basic categories**
Every expense is assigned to one of the built-in spending categories: food, transport, leisure, and more. Categories help you group your spending so patterns become visible at a glance.

**Monthly spending total**
The app calculates and displays the sum of all expenses recorded in the current month. The total updates immediately every time you add or remove an entry.

**Category chart**
A visual chart breaks your monthly spending into segments by category. You can see at a glance which categories are consuming the largest share of your budget.

**Browser localStorage persistence**
GastoClaro saves all expense data to your browser's localStorage. Your entries survive page refreshes and browser restarts on the same device without any manual saving or syncing.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open GastoClaro in your browser">
    GastoClaro runs entirely in your browser — no server or build step needed. Open your project folder and double-click `index.html`, or drag it into an open browser tab. The app loads immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first expense">
    Fill in the expense form: enter a description, amount, category, and date. Click **Add** to save. The expense appears in the list and the monthly total updates right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your monthly summary">
    Scroll down to see your total for the month and the category chart. Add a few more expenses across different categories to see the chart update in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
