GastoClaro Plus and Pro are planned future releases that build on the Free plan. They are designed for users who want multi-month history, budget tracking, data export, and deeper analysis of their spending habits. Neither plan is available yet — the current release is the Free plan MVP.

<Warning>
  Plus and Pro plans are not yet available. The current release is the free MVP. This page describes planned future features and estimated pricing only.
</Warning>

## Plan details

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Plus — $3–$5 / month">
    The Plus plan includes everything in the Free plan, and adds tools for managing your budget and reviewing your history over time.

    **User account**
    A personal account ties your expense data to a login so you can access it from multiple devices or browsers.

    **Multi-month expense history**
    Browse and search your expenses across any previous month, not just the current one.

    **Monthly budget**
    Set a spending limit for the month. GastoClaro tracks your progress against that limit throughout the month.

    **Visual budget alerts**
    When your spending approaches or exceeds your monthly budget, the app displays a visual alert so you can adjust before the month ends.

    **Export to PDF and Excel**
    Download your expense data as a PDF or Excel file for record-keeping, sharing, or use in other tools.

    **Estimated price:** $3–$5 USD per month
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pro — $8–$10 / month">
    The Pro plan includes everything in Plus, and adds intelligent analysis and household-level tracking.

    **Spending habit analysis**
    GastoClaro analyzes your expense patterns over time to surface trends in how and where you spend money.

    **Automatic recommendations**
    Based on your habits, the app suggests areas where you may be able to reduce spending.

    **Monthly comparisons**
    See how your spending in any given month compares to previous months, broken down by category.

    **Family mode**
    Share a single GastoClaro account across a household, with expenses visible to all members.

    **Estimated price:** $8–$10 USD per month
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will Plus and Pro be available?">
    There is no confirmed release date for Plus or Pro. Both plans are on the product roadmap as future development priorities. Check the roadmap page for the latest status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export my free plan data?">
    Data export is not included in the Free plan. PDF and Excel export is planned as a Plus feature. If you are using the Free plan today, your data is saved to localStorage in your browser and is not currently exportable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a family plan?">
    Family mode — which lets a household share one account and see all members' expenses — is planned for the Pro plan. It is not available in Free or Plus.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
