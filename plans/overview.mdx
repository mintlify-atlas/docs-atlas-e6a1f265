GastoClaro offers three plans designed to grow with your needs. The Free plan is fully available today and covers all the essentials for tracking your monthly spending. Plus and Pro are planned future releases that will add multi-month history, budgeting tools, data export, and advanced analysis.

<Info>
  Only the Free plan is currently available. Plus and Pro are planned for a future release.
</Info>
