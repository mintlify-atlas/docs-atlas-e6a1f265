---
title: "Record your first expense with GastoClaro in minutes"
description: "Follow this step-by-step guide to open GastoClaro, fill in the expense form, and view your monthly summary and category chart for the first time."
---

GastoClaro is designed to get you tracking expenses as fast as possible. There is nothing to install and no account to create — you open the app in your browser and you're ready to go. This guide walks you through recording your first expense and reviewing your spending summary.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open GastoClaro in your browser">
    GastoClaro runs entirely in your browser without any build step or server. To launch it, open the project folder and double-click `index.html`, or drag it into an open browser tab. The app loads immediately.

    <Info>
      If you have cloned the repository, the file is at the root of the project: `GastoClaro-Proyecto-Desarrollo-Web/index.html`. No local server is required.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the expense form">
    On the main page you will find the expense registration form. Complete each field:

    - **Description** — a short label for the expense, for example `Lunch` or `Bus ticket`.
    - **Amount** — the cost in your local currency, for example `8.50`.
    - **Category** — choose from the built-in options: food, transport, leisure, and more.
    - **Date** — the date the expense occurred. It defaults to today.

    All four fields are required. If you leave one blank, the form will prompt you to complete it before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add and confirm the entry">
    Click the **Add** button to save the expense. Two things happen immediately:

    1. The new expense appears in the expense list below the form, showing the description, category, amount, and date.
    2. The monthly total at the top of the page updates to include the new amount.

    You can delete any expense from the list at any time by clicking the remove button next to it. The total adjusts automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your monthly summary and category chart">
    Scroll down to see the monthly overview. The summary shows your total spending for the current month. Below it, the category chart breaks your expenses into segments — one per category — so you can see at a glance where most of your money is going.

    Add a few more expenses across different categories to see the chart update in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can close the browser tab and reopen `index.html` later — all your expenses will still be there. GastoClaro saves everything to your browser's localStorage automatically, so your data persists between sessions on the same device without any manual saving.
</Tip>
