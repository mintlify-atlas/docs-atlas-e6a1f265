## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open GastoClaro in your browser">
    GastoClaro runs entirely in your browser. Open `index.html` from the repository or navigate to the hosted version. No installation, no login required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first expense">
    Fill in the expense form with a description, amount, category, and date. Click **Add** to save it — the entry appears instantly in your expense list and your monthly total updates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your spending">
    Browse your categorized expense list and check the monthly summary. The visual chart breaks down spending by category so you can see exactly where your money is going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  GastoClaro stores all your data in your browser's localStorage. Your expenses are private, never sent to a server, and persist between sessions on the same device.
</Note>
